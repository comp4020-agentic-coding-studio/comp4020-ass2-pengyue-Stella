and structure, not plated-food styling — paired with SVG scientific
annotations drawn on top (axes, labels, callouts) rather than presented as

brief's "brittle spring-roll fracture" specimen: no on-topic spring-roll
cross-section photo turned up on Pexels in a quick search, and the fried,
fractured crust texture is the same material phenomenon.
