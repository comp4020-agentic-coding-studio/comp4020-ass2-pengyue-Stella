Every citation on this site was checked against a real publisher or journal
record before publication — see the [academic integrity
policy](/policies/#academic-integrity). This page is the complete list;
[Resources](/resources/) links to a short topic-grouped starting point drawn
from the same list for students who want a jumping-off point rather than a
full bibliography.

A citation appearing here does not mean every number attributed to it in a
module is a direct quotation or a measured value from that source — many
module figures are clearly labelled illustrative or synthetic, constructed
to sit within the ranges these sources establish (see each module's own
"Data provenance" note). The reference itself, and the general behaviour it
describes, are real.
