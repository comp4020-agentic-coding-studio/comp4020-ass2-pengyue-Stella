All twelve weeks, grouped into the same four stages as the modules. Modules
are the written teaching material; a lecture applies that material through a
demonstration, comparison or worked example rather than repeating it. Four —
weeks 1, 4, 7 and 10 — are **anchor lectures**: each opens its stage by
framing the work ahead, rather than following a single module, and each gets
the larger, bordered treatment below. Only Week 10 carries a real slide deck,
*Crispness Is a Metastable State*. `related:` connects every other lecture to
the module it follows.
