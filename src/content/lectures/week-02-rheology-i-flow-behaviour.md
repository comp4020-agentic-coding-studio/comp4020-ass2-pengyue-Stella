## Outline

- Recap: Module 1 gave us G′, G″ and tan δ for a material sitting still.
  This lecture asks the companion question — what happens once it flows?
- Live comparison of two flow curves: a Newtonian fluid (honey) against a
  yield-stress fluid (doubanjiang), read directly off shear stress vs.
  shear rate rather than described in words first.
- Building the Herschel-Bulkley equation term by term on the board, ending
  on the yield-stress term and why it is the one number that separates
  "thick" from "won't move until pushed."
- Naming shear-thinning and shear-thickening as the two ways a fluid's
  apparent viscosity can depend on how hard you push it, with a kitchen
  example of each.
- Preview of the Module 2 activity: fitting a real flow curve in the
  [Virtual Rheometer](/labs/virtual-rheometer/) and deciding which of the
  three models actually fits, not just which one runs.
- Forward pointer: Module 10 revisits this exact fitting problem with the
  tools to judge a fit rigorously, once residuals are on the table.
