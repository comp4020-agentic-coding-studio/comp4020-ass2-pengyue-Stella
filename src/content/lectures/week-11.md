## Outline

- Recap: Module 9 gave us a single glass-transition boundary, crossed once
  as a crust fries. This lecture reframes the same physics as a state
  diagram with a target *region* on it, not one boundary crossed once.
- Building the temperature-versus-moisture map live: a crisp region bounded
  above by temperature and, separately, bounded by moisture — two distinct
  ways to leave the crisp state, not one.
- Why a cracker left in humid air softens at constant room temperature:
  moisture uptake alone can cross the same glass-transition boundary that
  heat crosses in Module 9, from the other side.
- Using the [Crispness Phase Diagram](/labs/crispness-phase-diagram/) live
  to compare two specimens' stability margins — how much moisture or
  temperature headroom each one has before it leaves the crisp region.
- Setting up Module 12: this map gives a *target region*. The capstone asks
  which formulation or process choice actually moves a specimen toward it.
