Idris runs the laboratory-facing half of SLOP3358: the two virtual
instruments, the supplied datasets, and the weekly problem sets. Trained in
polymer physics before moving into food-materials characterisation, Idris
wrote the reference implementations behind the Virtual Rheometer and the
Crispness Phase Diagram and is the first point of contact for anything a
model gets visibly wrong.

**Consultation hours** are held immediately after each Tuesday module
session, in the same room — no booking needed. Outside of that, email with
the module number in the subject line and expect a reply within two working
days. Questions about a specific dataset or instrument reading are best
asked with a screenshot attached.

**Escalation path**: for anything about marking of the Laboratory Portfolio
or Modelling Problem Sets, ask Idris first; unresolved marking disputes, or
anything affecting the whole cohort, go to Marisol.
