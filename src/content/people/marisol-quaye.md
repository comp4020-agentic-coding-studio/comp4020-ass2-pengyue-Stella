Marisol designed SLOP3358's four-stage progression — measurement, structure,
transformation, prediction — after a decade split between rheology labs and
undergraduate teaching, and remains convinced that a food material is the
easiest way to make soft-matter physics feel consequential rather than
abstract. Marisol convenes the course, sets and moderates all four
assessments, and lectures the Stage 4 material on model fitting and inverse
design.

**Consultation hours** run Thursday afternoons by email-arranged
appointment; the fastest response is usually during the Tuesday session
itself. Email is for course-level questions — assessment scope, extensions,
the fit between a module and an assessment brief.

**Escalation path**: questions about a specific module's content or a
specific piece of marked work go to Idris first. Marisol handles anything
that changes what's expected of the whole cohort, and any dispute Idris
can't resolve.
