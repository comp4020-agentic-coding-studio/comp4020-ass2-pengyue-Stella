## The brief

> Four short problems, one drawn from each of Modules 5, 7, 8 and 9,
> applying the percolation, transport, kinetics and glass-transition models
> to new numbers you have not seen in the module pages.

This is a problem-solving exercise, not a report: each problem gives you a
scenario and a target quantity, and asks you to set up and solve the
relevant equation, showing your working. The problems intentionally reuse
the models rather than the food systems from their source modules, to check
that you can apply a model to an unfamiliar case rather than recall a
worked example.

## What you submit

A single PDF, one section per problem, with the governing equation stated,
the working shown, and the final answer given with correct units. Problem
sheets are released on the course site two weeks before the due date.

The marking weights working and reasoning almost as heavily as the final
numeric answer — a correct answer reached by the wrong method, or with no
visible reasoning, does not score as well as an honest, well-reasoned
attempt that arrives at a slightly wrong number.
